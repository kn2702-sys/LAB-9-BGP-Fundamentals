! PC-to-PC ping stops at the internal router. That is realistic: in a
! real enterprise the internal routers point a default route at their
! border router (the other standard fix is redistributing BGP into
! OSPF at the border). Apply the block for your router below, then
! re-run VERIFICATION step 4.
